## What Certificate of Analysis Generator does

Create an example certificate summarizing measured quality results for a selected product or shipment.

### A simple example

A Certificate of Analysis, often called a CoA, communicates test results and agreed specification limits to a customer. This demo shows how sample records can become a consistent report rather than requiring repeated manual copying.

### What you can do in this free demo

Review the built-in sample measurements, select the available product or shipment filters, and enter the demonstration company and report details. Review the generated summary before downloading the available Word or Excel document. No data or logo upload is available.

The data is synthetic and already included. You can explore the existing filters, settings, charts, and downloads without supplying company files. Changes affect your demonstration session, not production equipment or records.

### How to understand the results

A characteristic is something measured, such as a dimension. LSL and USL mean lower and upper specification limits. Target is the intended value. Summary statistics describe the selected measurements, and compliance indicators compare them with the configured limits. The underlying data includes Part, Characteristic, Value, LSL, Target, and USL.

### What the results cannot tell you

Every exported certificate is a demonstration using synthetic data, not an approved certificate for a real shipment. Typed names are not digital signatures. Records for the same characteristic must have consistent specifications because the summary uses the first specification values in that group. Review units, traceability, selection filters, and acceptance rules before issuing a real CoA.

### What a version customized for you would need

Approved quality records, consistent specification and unit definitions, lot or shipment identifiers, your report layout, branding, approval requirements, and retention rules. We can tailor the output to your customer requirements and approved release process.
