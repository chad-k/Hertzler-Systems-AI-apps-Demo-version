## What Process Optimization does

Explore which operating settings a fitted model suggests might bring a measurement closer to its target.

### A simple example

If temperature, pressure, and speed vary, the resulting dimension may vary too. This demo uses synthetic examples to estimate those relationships and propose settings within the chosen bounds.

### What you can do in this free demo

Load or reset the bundled demo data if needed. Review the column mapping, select the measurement and process inputs, choose the part and machine groups, and check allowed input ranges. Run the available modeling and optimization controls. Compare predicted measurements at the suggested settings with the target and specification limits.

The data is synthetic and already included. You can explore the existing filters, settings, charts, and downloads without supplying company files. Changes affect your demonstration session, not production equipment or records.

### How to understand the results

The implemented linear model estimates a measurement from the selected inputs for each part-machine group. Optimization searches allowed values to minimize squared distance between the predicted measurement and the target. At least ten usable rows are needed per group. Bounds limit the search but do not establish that every combination is physically safe.

### What the results cannot tell you

A suggested setting is a model-based experiment candidate, not an instruction to change equipment. Associations are not proof of causation, and in-sample fit does not establish performance on future runs. Nonlinear behavior, omitted factors, or unrealistic combinations can invalidate a recommendation. The synthetic fixture illustrates the workflow only.

### What a version customized for you would need

Historical settings and resulting measurements, part and machine identifiers, specifications and targets, valid operating bounds, and engineering constraints. We would validate on held-out runs and use an approved trial plan before any real process adjustment.
