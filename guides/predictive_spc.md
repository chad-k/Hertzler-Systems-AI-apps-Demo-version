## What Predictive SPC does

See whether recent process behavior may be warning of a future quality problem. SPC means statistical process control: using measurements over time to understand variation.

### A simple example

Imagine measuring a part every few minutes. Most measurements may be acceptable today, but a developing trend could increase the chance of a future out-of-specification result. This demo learns patterns from synthetic examples and estimates that risk.

### What you can do in this free demo

Select a demonstration scenario and the available analysis settings. Explore the time-series charts, model results, and what-if controls. Compare the baseline with a changed setting and look at the estimated risk for the selected future window.

The data is synthetic and already included. You can explore the existing filters, settings, charts, and downloads without supplying company files. Changes affect your demonstration session, not production equipment or records.

### How to understand the results

A specification limit describes what the customer or design accepts. A control limit describes the process behavior estimated from data. They answer different questions. A risk probability is the model’s estimate, not a promise about the next part. The app also looks for selected statistical patterns; it does not implement every possible SPC rule.

### What the results cannot tell you

The classifier uses a chronological training/test split to illustrate prediction. Small datasets, changing operating conditions, imbalanced events, and overlapping future windows can make performance look better than it will be in production. What-if effects show model associations, not proven cause and effect. Synthetic results are not validation on your process.

### What a version customized for you would need

Measurements with timestamps, part and equipment identifiers, specification limits, relevant operating settings, and agreed definitions of future failures. We would agree on the prediction horizon, evaluate unseen production periods, and integrate useful alerts.
