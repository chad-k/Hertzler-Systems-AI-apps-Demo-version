## What Operator Data Integrity Checker does

Find measurement-entry patterns that deserve a closer look before you rely on the data.

### A simple example

Measurements entered extremely quickly, in repeated sequences, or long after an inspection may indicate a workflow or data-collection issue. This demo brings those patterns together so a reviewer can ask informed questions.

### What you can do in this free demo

Explore the generated example records and configure the available rule settings. Run the checks, review the summary and individual flags, then compare patterns by the available operator and time fields. Use the downloads for demonstration review.

The data is synthetic and already included. You can explore the existing filters, settings, charts, and downloads without supplying company files. Changes affect your demonstration session, not production equipment or records.

### How to understand the results

The app uses explicit weighted checks rather than a trained fraud model. Checks include rapid entry, grouped or backfilled entries, repeated or rounded values, exact-target values, zeros, and unusually low variation. The combined score indicates how strongly the configured rules apply.

### What the results cannot tell you

A high score is not proof of falsification, misconduct, or poor work. Legitimate automation, rounding, repeated production, time-zone errors, or an instrument’s resolution may produce the same patterns. Review the source process and speak with relevant staff before drawing conclusions.

### What a version customized for you would need

Entry timestamps, numeric measurements, operator identifiers, and optionally actual inspection timestamps, targets, and supporting production context. We would agree on fair thresholds, investigate legitimate exceptions, and define who reviews each type of flag.
