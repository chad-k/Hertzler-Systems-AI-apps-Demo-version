## What Smart Inspection Frequency does

Explore how process capability, drift, and recent warning signals can inform a proposed inspection interval.

### A simple example

Frequent inspections use time and resources, but infrequent inspections may allow an issue to go unnoticed. This demo illustrates how different process behaviors can lead to different suggested intervals.

### What you can do in this free demo

Use the bundled sample measurements and standards. Check grouping fields, measurement columns, and the preselected specification columns. Review the settings, run the calculation, and compare the proposed intervals and contributing measures across process groups.

The data is synthetic and already included. You can explore the existing filters, settings, charts, and downloads without supplying company files. Changes affect your demonstration session, not production equipment or records.

### How to understand the results

A subgroup is a set of measurements collected together. Capability compares estimated spread and centering with specifications. Drift describes movement over time. Rule flags and recorded failures can increase the risk estimate and shorten the proposed interval. An interval is the suggested time between checks, not the time required to carry out one inspection.

### What the results cannot tell you

The interval is a configurable heuristic, not a proven guarantee of acceptable quality. The app combines drift, risk, a safety factor, and flags, then applies minimum/maximum bounds. Groups with too little history are limited; rule flags are suppressed with fewer than twenty subgroups. Missing failure records cannot prove there were no failures. Fixed contractual, regulatory, or customer inspection requirements still need to be respected.

### What a version customized for you would need

Time-stamped subgroups, part and equipment identifiers, correct standards and subgroup sizes, failure records, and existing inspection requirements. We can calibrate assumptions to your risk tolerance and validate proposed intervals before changing an inspection plan.
