## What SPC Auto Interpretation does

Turn control-chart signals into explanations that help someone understand what a process may be doing.

### A simple example

A process can vary randomly or show a systematic change. A chart may show a point far from normal, a run on one side of the average, or a trend. This demo explains selected patterns and offers investigation ideas.

### What you can do in this free demo

Choose the demonstration scenario, subgroup size, and rule set. Review the measurement chart and any detected signals. Compare the explanation with the scenario and explore the available cause and action suggestions.

The data is synthetic and already included. You can explore the existing filters, settings, charts, and downloads without supplying company files. Changes affect your demonstration session, not production equipment or records.

### How to understand the results

A subgroup is a small set of measurements collected together. The center line represents typical behavior; control limits estimate expected process variation. Nelson, Western Electric, and other rule choices look for different patterns. A signal means the pattern warrants attention, not that a specific root cause has been established.

### What the results cannot tell you

The AIAG option is simplified. Suggested causes and actions are illustrative guidance based on demo context or generic patterns. Control limits are not engineering tolerances, and a stable process can still make unacceptable parts. Rules should be selected and validated for the production context.

### What a version customized for you would need

Time-ordered measurements, subgroup definitions, part and machine context, approved rule choices, and useful event history such as tool changes or maintenance. We can adapt explanations and response workflows to your terminology.
